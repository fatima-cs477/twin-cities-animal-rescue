Adoption Process Overview

We work to match pets with the right families to ensure lifelong success. View our adoptable pets below:

  1. Submit an adoption application online.
  2. Meet with an adoption counselor to discuss pet compatibility.
  3. Schedule a meet-and-greet with the pet.
  4. Finalize adoption paperwork and bring your new family member home!

Foster Program Overview

Foster care provides temporary homes for animals needing medical recovery, socialization, or a break from shelter life. We supply all necessary food, crates, and medical care—you supply the love!

Volunteer Roles

Volunteers keep our shelter running smoothly. Available opportunities include:

  • Dog Walker: Provide daily exercise and outdoor enrichment.
  • Cat Matchmaker: Assist shelter visitors with cat interactions.
  • Event Support: Help organize and run community adoption events.
