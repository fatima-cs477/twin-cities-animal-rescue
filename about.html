Our Story

Founded in 2012, Twin Cities Animal Rescue began as a small network of dedicated foster homes. Over the past decade, we have grown into a full community resource center, rescuing thousands of pets across the metropolitan area.

A Message from Our Director

Core Values

  • Compassion: Treating every animal and human with empathy and respect.
  • Integrity: Maintaining complete transparency in our operations and funding.
  • Community: Collaborating with local partners to expand animal welfare education.

Partner Acknowledgments
